Current work first. All of it is C++, most of it in Unreal Engine.

SPORTS SIMS

4PEOPLE · SENIOR SOFTWARE ENGINEER MAR '25 – NOW

Two sports simulation titles with broadcast-style presentation, on a team of 40+. Both are under NDA, so there is no footage here, only what I built.

TENNIS · UE 5.8

  • In-game cinematics system: three plugins plus game code, about 10,000 lines of C++ in the first delivery. Server commands start Level Sequences through StateTree tasks.
  • Custom Sequencer track with its own track editor: keys on the timeline reach gameplay code as tagged events.
  • Rigid-body anim node that detects the teleport at a camera cut, so hair physics keeps its pose instead of snapping.
  • Gameplay camera plugin with handoff between cinematics and gameplay.

VOLLEYBALL 6V6 · UE 5.6

  • Camera operator plugin: rule-based behavior, ball tracking without gimbal lock. The rest of the camera system runs on it.
  • Rally replay: full rallies recorded and played back deterministically, with the RNG state captured.
  • Court-side AI on StateTree in C++: coaches, line referees, extras, avoidance based on the player's predicted path.
  • Server-driven match flow: serve, end-of-rally reactions, side changes, timeouts. Match commentary with phrase conditions on rally events (shared ownership).

On the volleyball title: about 550 commits in 20 months, top five of 40+ contributors by commit count.

THE CRUST

VEOM STUDIO · SENIOR SOFTWARE ENGINEER JAN '20 – MAR '24

Survival-factory game on the Moon, released on Steam. Four years as one of the core developers.

  • Frame rate from 4 FPS to 60+ (frame time from 250 ms to 16.6 ms) with multithreading and custom HLSL shaders.
  • Conveyor system that processes 50,000+ resources in under 2 ms, multithreaded.
  • Save system: save and load times cut by 90%.
  • Custom rendering pipeline for resource visualization: it doubled the frame rate on the Vulkan renderer.
  • In-game market with dynamic prices driven by player actions.
